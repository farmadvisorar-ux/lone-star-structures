Roof Accessories

How it works

Gutters matter more on steel than people expect. A metal roof sheds water fast and concentrates it at the eave, and without gutters that volume lands in one line against the foundation. In snow country, retention devices keep accumulated snow from releasing over a doorway all at once.

Key points

  • Ridge ventilation
  • Gutters and downspouts
  • Snow retention for cold climates
  • Curbs for roof-mounted equipment
